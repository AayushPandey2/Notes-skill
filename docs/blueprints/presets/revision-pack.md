# Variant: Multi-Note Exam Revision Pack

- **Purpose:** Synthesize an existing multi-note vault topic folder into one unified exam-review pack without copying paragraphs verbatim.
- **Parent Master:** [M7 — Synthesis & Revision](../formats/synthesis-revision.md) (`revision_pack`)
- **Activation Trigger:** `"revision pack"`, `"exam revision"`, `"consolidate topic notes"` via `/scribe-notes`.
- **Inputs & Dependencies:** Existing vault notes, sibling notes (`[[Note Title]]`), verified source citations; [revision-and-linking.md](../../procedures/updating/revision-and-linking.md).
- **Execution Overrides:**
  1. Draw definitions, formulas, and comparisons exclusively from verified vault notes (`[[Note Title]]`) or verified sources—never invent external claims.
  2. Organize into: (a) Big-picture conceptual progression & N-step mental model, (b) Grouped formula & condition cheat sheet linked to full derivations, (c) Side-by-side method comparison table, (d) `Mistake | Correct approach` traps + Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced Q&A cues.
  3. Compress into retrieval cues; link back to canonical note headings rather than pasting verbatim prose.
- **Failure Handling & Validation:** Enforce formula assumption retention and wikilink resolution ([verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md)).
