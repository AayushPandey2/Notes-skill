# M1 — Comprehensive Theory Notes

- **Master ID:** `M1` (`deep_notes`)
- **Primary intent:** `comprehensive_learning` — deliver a complete, self-contained beginner-to-advanced learning chapter from motivation and essential foundations through simpler baselines, mechanisms, mathematical tools, concrete reasoning, major variants/tasks, practical usage, limitations, comparisons, and authoritative references (never a condensed technical reference for someone who already understands the topic).
- **Activation signals:** `"teach me from scratch"`, `"explain deeply"`, `"comprehensive notes"`, `"from fundamentals to advanced"`, or bare `/scribe-notes <topic>` without an explicit alternate format.
- **Non-activation:** Do not select `M1` when the user primarily requests a compact cheat sheet (`M3`), solved problem set (`M4`), implementation guide (`M5`), quiz/flashcards (`M6`), or standalone comparison/revision pack (`M7`).
- **Load as needed:** [teaching rules](../../../core/teaching_core.md), [theory quality contract](../../../core/theory_quality_contract.md), [math guidance](../../../core/math_gates.md), [visual rules](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md), [adaptive planning](../../procedures/authoring/adaptive-engine.md), [source fidelity](../../procedures/sourcing/source-fidelity.md), [authoring rules](../../../core/authoring_rules.md), and the primary subject blueprint in [subject-registry.yaml](../../dispatch/subject-registry.yaml).
- **Output profile:** Comprehensive collections use the five-key YAML profile in [properties policy](../../properties/properties-policy.md); single or existing notes use Standard Obsidian Properties without overwriting user-managed fields.

## 1. Opening Roadmap (Intuition First, Purpose & TOC)

Open cleanly with line-1 YAML and H1 title, then establish plain-language intuition and a concrete everyday scenario *before* introducing formal jargon definitions or a large navigation block. Follow that opening intuition with concise orientation (without repeating YAML `difficulty` verbatim as a visible dump) and a compact **clickable Obsidian Table of Contents** (`- [[#1. Heading|Short Label]]`). Apply the three-tier prerequisite policy in [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md): never let a `Prerequisites:` list or link replace teaching an essential unmastered local foundation.

## 2. Progressive Explanation Flow & Adaptive Learning Regions

Load the matching knowledge-type subtemplate (`conceptual`, `mathematical`, `computational`, `scientific`, `empirical`, or `historical`) and primary subject profile (`docs/blueprints/subjects/`). Let headings and structure emerge naturally from the subject rather than forcing identical headings or ML templates onto unrelated fields. Organically weave whichever of these **adaptive learning regions** serve the topic (never forcing every note to expose all of them as rigid top-level headings, and integrating mathematics, worked examples, and adjacent visuals inline where they teach best): *Orientation · Foundations · Core theory · Formal understanding · Visual understanding · Worked understanding · Practical understanding · Advanced understanding · Critical understanding · Mastery and revision · References*.

1. **One Conceptual Burden at a Time (Opening):** Build the intuition that makes the formal definition meaningful before introducing dense technical terminology (never stuffing advanced claims into an opening callout).
2. **Teach the Simpler Baseline & Local Prerequisites:** Teach essential building blocks and mathematical tools (e.g., simpler baselines, local slopes/curvature/Taylor approximations) before the advanced model builds on them. For key definitions, establish the need, state a concise definition, translate to ordinary language, provide a concrete example beside it, and state the nearest boundary—separating definition, consequence, assumption, and optional advanced property.
3. **Domain-Appropriate Mechanism & Example Portfolio:** Carry one consistent **running example, case, system, or evidence anchor** through the explanation, distributing verified existing web-sourced visuals across relevant sections with before/image/caption/after teaching per [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) (never generating images) and selectively adding *minimal, discriminating, boundary, counterexample,* or *transfer* examples where each clarifies a distinct point.
4. **Limitations of Simpler Baselines & Conceptual Surprise:** Show concretely why a naive or idealized baseline fails before introducing the richer mechanism or model.
5. **Continuous mathematical reasoning:** Use [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) to order reasoning units by dependency. Let visible prose and milestone equations develop the argument, then complete each central derivation locally in the visible argument or in a descriptive formula fold below the principal result. Apply the canonical-reference rules in the math policy to prerequisites and reused results. Explain what the result lets the reader do next.
6. **Consequential Transitions, Result Interpretation & Micro-Checks:** Trace consequential state, logical, physical, or causal transitions step by step, interpret what the resulting outcome establishes (and does not establish), and optionally include a brief local reconstruction check or assumption-change analysis where helpful.
7. **Assumptions, Evidence, Validity Boundaries & Checkpoints:** Distinguish epistemic claim types ([theory_quality_contract.md](../../../core/theory_quality_contract.md)), qualify strong claims, state *"What It Does NOT Assume/Establish"* boundaries, and place visible `> [!question] Concept Checkpoint` blocks (with nested `> > [!success]- Reveal Answer`) where active checking adds value.

## 3. Closing & Companion Handoff

End cleanly with a concise **Mental Model** grounded strictly in concepts actually taught earlier in the note (when it aids reconstruction rather than repeating headings), a trailing **Notation Reference** *only when quantitative/symbolic notation warrants one* (with every key symbol also defined locally where first used), a concise **References** section citing primary authoritative sources (foundational papers, version-qualified official documentation, canonical textbooks) for independent non-strict notes, and reciprocal wikilinks to `<note stem> - Experimental Lab.md` and `<note stem> - Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A.md` **only after verifying those companion files are in the resolved `artifact_plan` and actually published** (per [adaptive-engine.md](../../procedures/authoring/adaptive-engine.md) and `artifact_policy` in [master-registry.yaml](../../dispatch/master-registry.yaml))—never leave unverified placeholder companion links.

Do **not** duplicate companion formula sheets, tiered interview/viva Q&A, or worked problem packs inside `theory` when `mastery_pack` is in the resolved `artifact_plan`. Review against [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md) before saving; for multi-chapter `M1` collections, follow [composition-contract.md](../../procedures/authoring/composition-contract.md).

Plan complete learning units around questions the reader needs to answer. Give core mechanisms room for a concrete setup, step-by-step change, and interpretation. Use that visible teaching path to organize prose, examples, equations and figures before adding supporting callouts or assessments. If resource limits require partitioning, finish coherent sections and disclose outstanding scope.
