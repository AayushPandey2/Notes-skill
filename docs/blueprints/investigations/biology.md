# Biology Investigation Lab Blueprint

Use this lean, realization-focused blueprint for Biology companion labs (`<note stem> - Experimental Lab.md`). The [shared lab contract](../../procedures/authoring/experimental-lab.md) and [physical lab safety policy](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md) govern structure, collapsed **A–G** walkthroughs, safe scope, and honest evidence.

**Do not** fabricate wet-lab observations, prescribe unsupervised handling of biological agents/pathogens, or force irrelevant ML/4-language programming stacks onto biology topics.

### Shared Biological System & Scale Contract
Define the biological scale (molecular, cellular, organismal, population), entities/components, environmental variables, and evidence basis (**Published**, **Illustrative**, or **Analytical**).

## Four Biology Tracks (Apply Track-Applicability Gate)

1. **Track 1 — Structure & Mechanism:** Trace molecular/cellular structures, binding/catalysis, pathways, or regulatory interactions step by step.
2. **Track 2 — Observation & Evidence:** Interpret published experimental curves, microscopy/diagram features, or genetic/population datasets without inventing observations.
3. **Track 3 — Controlled Investigation:** Analyze a safe variable-response relationship (e.g., substrate concentration, inhibitor effect, selection pressure) or hypothetical experimental design with controls.
4. **Track 4 — Biological Variation & Disruption:** Examine regulation, feedback failure, mutation/knockout effects, and model assumptions (e.g., Michaelis–Menten steady-state breakdown).

Directly beneath each realization, include `> [!example]- Understand this realization: biology, mechanism, and evidence dry run` covering **A–G**: **A** biological system, components, conditions; **B** governing biochemical/physiological/evolutionary mechanism; **C** step-by-step causal sequence; **D** concrete worked trace (rate calculation, Punnett/linkage cross, or pathway state trace); **E** functional interpretation & what data do not prove; **F** disrupted regulation, model breakdown, or experimental confounds; **G** core-mechanism reconstruction.

## Verification Status

State result status honestly (**Published** / **Analytical** / **Illustrative**, never invented **Observed** wet-lab data); if modeling code is present, end with **`Source and static review only; not compiled or executed here.`**
