# Subtemplate: Empirical

- **Purpose:** Knowledge-type strategy for **empirical studies, experimental methodology, benchmarks, research papers, and statistical evidence** across sciences, engineering, social sciences, and ML—keeping design, measurement, uncertainty, and threats to validity crystal clear.
- **Activation conditions:** Paper analyses, empirical studies, experiments, benchmarks, ablations, or uncertainty evaluation (`research`).
- **Expected inputs:** Research question, population/sample or experimental setup, concrete data/reported table, local metric/variable definitions, source claims with locators, controls/baselines, uncertainty (`SD`/`SE`/`CI`), and validity threats.
- **Teaching Strategy:** Frame the empirical question and stakes → present a concrete sample/setup and clarify metric/variable conventions → explain why naive metrics or uncontrolled observations mislead → build local statistical/measurement foundations with tiny numerical checks → walk through experimental protocol and matched controls/baselines → decode actual source figures and uncertainty bars at inflection points → analyze confounders, missing controls/ablations, replication status, and **what the data do NOT establish** → close with synthesis and links to any generated companions.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected source figures, experimental setups, and reported curves/error bars, plus Markdown tables for exact reported values.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Explain metrics/tests via [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md); cite exact locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if missing); never present illustrative datasets or correlation as causal empirical proof via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
