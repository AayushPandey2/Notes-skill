# Subtemplate: Empirical

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for research papers, benchmarks, empirical studies, and statistical evaluations—making metrics, protocols, uncertainty, and source figures crystal clear in short connected prose.
- **Activation conditions:** Paper breakdowns, benchmark evaluations, ablations, experimental methodology, or uncertainty analysis (`research`).
- **Expected inputs:** Research question, population/sample, tiny illustrative/reported row table, upfront **Metric & Symbol Dictionary**, source claims with locators, baselines, uncertainty (`SD`/`SE`/`CI`), and validity threats.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs at each conceptual inflection point. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Frame the empirical question + real-world stakes table (Layer 1) → tiny concrete sample table + upfront **Metric & Statistical Symbol Dictionary** (noting convention clashes like Accuracy vs Balanced Accuracy or F1 vs AUC) (Layer 2) → show concretely why naive metrics (e.g., raw accuracy on imbalanced data) fail (Layer 3) → local foundations explaining metrics, probability calibration, and uncertainty from scratch with tiny numerical examples (Layer 4) → walk through protocol pipeline & matched-baseline controls (Layer 5) → decode actual source figures, ROC/PR curves, and error bars at inflection points (Layers 6–7) → check concrete calculation on one shared confusion-matrix/table fixture and verify metric formulas (Layers 8–9) → analyze confounders, missing ablations, replication status, and **what the data do NOT establish** (Layers 10–11) → close with synthesis and reciprocal links (Layer 12) to the companion lab and companion Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A file (Metric Selection Cheat Sheet `Situation | Recommended metric`, tap-to-reveal Tiered Q&A, and worked metric/validity checks).
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using actual inspected source figures, experimental setups, and reported benchmark/ablation plots, plus Markdown tables for exact reported values.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Explain metrics/tests via [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md); cite exact locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if missing); never present illustrative fixtures or correlation as causal empirical proof via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
