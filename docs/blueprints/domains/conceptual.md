# Subtemplate: Conceptual

- **Purpose:** Realize [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for concepts, definitions, mechanisms, comparisons, and general/non-quantitative domains through progressive explanation (one conceptual burden at a time, concrete examples and non-examples beside definitions, and comparison tables placed after explaining compared ideas).
- **Activation conditions:** Conceptual mastery, definitions, classifications, comparative choices, or general domain explanations (`conceptual`, `comparative`).
- **Expected inputs:** Concept identity, learner's problem, three-tier prerequisite plan, source policy, tiny concrete opening example, non-examples/counterexamples, and boundary conditions.

- **Realizing the Progressive Flow:** Open with plain-language intuition and a concrete everyday scenario before separated orientation/TOC when comprehensive ([authoring_rules.md](../../../core/authoring_rules.md)) → build local essential prerequisites ([teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md)) → introduce one conceptual burden at a time in ordinary language with concrete examples and discriminating non-examples beside definitions → trace `Starting state → operation → reason → intermediate change → outcome` (or formal arguments, premises, objections, and counterexamples in **Philosophy & Humanities**; incentives, equilibria, and empirical limits without presenting conditional models as universal guarantees in **Economics & Finance**) alongside structural/contrast visuals → distinguish facts from interpretations and carry an illustrative example through assumptions, *"What this does NOT require/mean"*, `Mistake | Correct approach`, and explained multi-option comparisons → close with a concise **Mental Model** and links to any generated companions.
- **Domain expectations (General & other domains):** Use domain-appropriate concepts, evidence, mechanisms, procedures, or comparisons (history remains optional); never force mathematical or computational machinery onto unrelated subjects.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected visuals strictly from supplied source figures or verified existing educational web raster figures for structural contrasts, classifications, taxonomies, and causal flows where they clarify the concept (respecting source-only limits; never generate images, custom SVG, Mermaid, or ASCII graphs).
- **Evidence, practice & failure handling:** Cite locators when source-bound (`[UNVERIFIED]` if unconfirmed); repair compressed openings, comparison tables before explanations, dense walls of prose, or links replacing essential local explanation via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
