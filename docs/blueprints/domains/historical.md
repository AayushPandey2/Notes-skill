# Subtemplate: Historical

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for historical sequences, primary evidence, structural causes, and historiographical debate without false determinism or forced technical machinery.
- **Activation conditions:** Historical analysis, institutional evolution, chronology-centered topics, or origin/context studies (`historical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Earlier setting, actors/institutions, verified dates and primary/secondary sources with locators, turning points, causal arguments, and competing scholarly accounts.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs of each substantive concept. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Establish the earlier setting and tensions (Layers 1–2) → plain-language summary and unpacked period vocabulary/institutions (Layers 3–5) → decode maps, archival artifacts, or period diagrams (Layer 6) → trace verified chronology and causal turning points without hindsight determinism (Layers 7–8) → ground broad forces in one concrete primary-source episode (Layer 9) → contrast competing scholarly interpretations and structural limits (Layers 10–11) → test causal and source-evaluation reasoning independently (Layer 12).
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster maps, archival artifacts, and period charts, plus native Mermaid only when chronological/institutional density warrants.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Never force equations or code onto non-quantitative history; verify dates/quotes with locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if uncertain); repair anachronisms or collapsed scholarly disputes via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
