# Subtemplate: Historical

- **Purpose:** Realize the progressive teaching flow in [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for historical sequences, primary evidence, structural causes, and historiographical debate—using short crisp 1–3 sentence paragraphs, concrete timeline tables, unpacked institutional terms, and primary visuals without forced math or code.
- **Activation conditions:** Historical analysis, institutional evolution, chronology-centered topics, or origin/context studies (`historical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Earlier setting, actors/institutions, upfront **Term & Actor Glossary**, verified dates and primary/secondary sources with locators, turning points, causal arguments, and competing scholarly accounts.

- **Realizing the Progressive Flow:** Frame the historical puzzle + snapshot of the period → concrete chronological overview table + local definitions of offices/terms clarifying anachronistic labels (reserving any full glossary for the end) → explain why single-cause textbook explanations fail → local institutional/background context needed before events unfold → trace causal sequence (`Earlier tension → trigger → institutional response → structural shift → consequence`) alongside decoded primary maps, archival artifacts, or period diagrams → ground broad forces in one concrete primary-source episode with source evaluation → contrast competing historiographical interpretations, boundary limits, and **"What this development did NOT cause"** → close with a concise causal mental model and links to any generated companions.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using relevant inspected local web raster maps, archival artifacts, period charts, and institutional diagrams where they clarify geography, chronology, or institutions (respecting source-only limits); never use Mermaid or ASCII graphs.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Never force equations or code onto non-quantitative history; verify dates/quotes with locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if uncertain); repair hindsight determinism or collapsed scholarly disputes via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
