# Subtemplate: Historical

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for historical sequences, primary evidence, structural causes, and historiographical debate—using short crisp 1–3 sentence paragraphs, concrete timeline tables, unpacked institutional terms, and primary visuals without forced math or code.
- **Activation conditions:** Historical analysis, institutional evolution, chronology-centered topics, or origin/context studies (`historical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Earlier setting, actors/institutions, upfront **Term & Actor Glossary**, verified dates and primary/secondary sources with locators, turning points, causal arguments, and competing scholarly accounts.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs at each conceptual inflection point. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Frame the historical puzzle + snapshot of the period (Layer 1) → concrete chronological overview table + upfront **Actors, Offices & Terms Glossary** clarifying anachronistic labels (Layer 2) → explain why single-cause textbook explanations fail (Layer 3) → local institutional/background context needed before events unfold (Layer 4) → trace causal sequence (`Earlier tension → trigger → institutional response → structural shift → consequence`) alongside decoded primary maps, archival artifacts, or period diagrams (Layers 5–7) → ground broad forces in one concrete primary-source episode with source evaluation (Layers 8–9) → contrast competing historiographical interpretations, boundary limits, and **"What this development did NOT cause"** (Layers 10–11) → close with Chronology/Cause Quick Reference, Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced source-analysis Q&A, and a concise causal mental model (Layer 12).
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster maps, archival artifacts, and period charts, plus native Mermaid only when chronological/institutional density warrants.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Never force equations or code onto non-quantitative history; verify dates/quotes with locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if uncertain); repair hindsight determinism or collapsed scholarly disputes via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
