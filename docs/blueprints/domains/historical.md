# Subtemplate: Historical

- **Purpose:** Realize the progressive teaching flow in [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for historical sequences, primary evidence, structural causes, and historiographical debate—using clear paragraphs organized by logical teaching move ([authoring_rules.md](../../../core/authoring_rules.md)), concrete timeline tables, unpacked institutional terms, and primary visuals without forced math or code.
- **Activation conditions:** Historical analysis, institutional evolution, chronology-centered topics, or origin/context studies (`historical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Earlier setting, actors/institutions, local term/actor definitions at first use (with an optional trailing **Term & Actor Glossary**), verified dates and primary/secondary sources with locators, turning points, causal arguments, and competing scholarly accounts.

- **Realizing the Progressive Flow:** Frame the historical puzzle + snapshot of the period $\to$ concrete chronological overview table + local definitions of offices/terms clarifying anachronistic labels (without reducing history to disconnected date lists, reserving any full glossary for the end) $\to$ explain why single-cause textbook explanations fail $\to$ local institutional/social/individual context needed before events unfold $\to$ trace causal sequence (`Earlier tension -> trigger -> institutional response -> structural shift -> consequence`) alongside decoded primary maps, historical photos, timelines, or archival artifacts $\to$ ground broad forces in one concrete primary-source episode with source evaluation $\to$ contrast competing historiographical interpretations, boundary limits, and **"What this development did NOT cause"** $\to$ close with a concise causal mental model and links to any generated companions.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using relevant inspected maps, archival photographs/artifacts, timelines, and institutional diagrams strictly from supplied source figures or verified existing educational web raster figures where they clarify geography, chronology, or institutions (respecting source-only limits; never generate images, custom SVG, Mermaid, or ASCII graphs).
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Never force equations or code onto non-quantitative history; verify dates/quotes with locators (`[UNVERIFIED]` if uncertain); repair hindsight determinism or collapsed scholarly disputes via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
