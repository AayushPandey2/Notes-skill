# Subtemplate: Mathematical

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for theorems, derivations, quantitative relations, and proofs by bridging intuition and geometry into rigorous symbols, transformations, and checks.
- **Activation conditions:** Displayed equations, formal derivations, proofs, quantitative modeling, or worked numerical problems (`mathematical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Governing equations, symbol/domain/unit specs, 3-tier prerequisites, assumptions, non-obvious transformation steps, and one canonical numerical fixture.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs of each substantive concept. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Motivate what question the math answers (Layer 1) → intuitive foothold and plain-language meaning (Layers 2–3) → unpack symbols, domains, and types (Layers 4–5) → decode geometric/function plots (Layer 6) → trace the transformation or proof mechanism (Layers 7–8) → work one canonical numerical example completely and interpret what the result establishes (Layer 9) → test counterexamples, edge cases, and generalization (Layers 10–11) → progress to independent checks (Layer 12).
- **Domain expectations (Mathematics):** Prioritize definitions, examples, assumptions, derivations or proofs, counterexamples, and generalization; explicitly distinguish **numerical evidence** from **proof**, and **approximation**, **identity**, **assumption**, and **theorem**.
- **Prerequisite bridges:** Define every symbol, index, quantifier, and domain restriction locally before first use.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster plots for geometry, function behavior, distributions, and transformations.
- **Formalism & worked examples:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) (`$$...$$` + `> [!note]- 📐 Understand this formula`) without repeating the same worked calculation across sections or creating separate visible headings for pronunciation/symbols/dimensions.
- **Evidence, practice & failure handling:** Distinguish source derivations from added bridges; include derivation, boundary, and transfer checks with collapsed answers; repair naked equations, skipped transformations, or raw LaTeX outside delimiters via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
