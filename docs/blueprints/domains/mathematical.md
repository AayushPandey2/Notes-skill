# Subtemplate: Mathematical

- **Purpose:** Realize [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) and the Mandatory Derivation Contract in [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) for theorems, derivations, quantitative relations, and proofs—building every prerequisite mathematical identity and tool from scratch with small concrete examples, required scalar-to-vector-to-matrix construction where vectorized, and explicit step-by-step transitions.

- **Expected inputs:** Governing equations, mathematical dependency order (prerequisite identities derived before downstream equations), progressive symbol introductions (with trailing **Notation Reference** when needed), local foundation tools, assumptions, full derivation transitions, and one consistent running example.

Use explicit transitions (never `"rearranging gives"` leaps), `aligned`/`cases` formatting, explicit distinction between **definition, assumption, theorem, identity, approximation, numerical illustration, and formal proof**, and close with a concise synthesis, optional Notation Reference, and links to any generated companions.
