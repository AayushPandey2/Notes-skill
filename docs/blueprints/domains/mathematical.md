# Subtemplate: Mathematical

- **Purpose:** Realize [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) and [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) for theorems, derivations, quantitative relations, and proofs—building every prerequisite mathematical tool from scratch with small concrete examples, scalar-to-vector progression where appropriate, and explicit step-by-step transitions.
- **Activation conditions:** Displayed equations, formal derivations, proofs, quantitative modeling, or worked numerical problems (`mathematical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Governing equations, progressive symbol introductions (with trailing **Notation Reference**), local foundation tools, assumptions, full derivation transitions, and one consistent running example.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs at each conceptual inflection point. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the Conceptual Flow:** Open with separated `Difficulty / Prerequisites / Learning objectives` + clickable TOC → introduce one conceptual burden at a time with concrete intuition → local prerequisite foundations → early worked example introducing symbols progressively (reserving the full Notation Reference for the end) → limitations of simpler rules → concept-appropriate math depth per [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) (bridge before formula, explicit transitions with no `"rearranging gives"` leaps, `> [!example]- Understand this formula step by step` beneath every displayed `$$...$$` block, `aligned` derivations, `cases` where needed) → explicit distinction between **definition, assumption, identity, approximation, numerical illustration, and formal proof** → counterexamples and boundaries → **Summary**, trailing **Notation Reference**, and reciprocal links to the companion lab and companion Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A file.
- **Domain expectations (Mathematics):** Prioritize definitions, assumptions, derivations or proofs, counterexamples, and generalization; adapt formula walkthroughs to proof statements without forcing artificial numerical substitutions.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster plots at inflection points (foundation curves alongside tables, function + derivative pairs, before/after geometric transforms, and constraint regions).
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md); distinguish source derivations from added bridges; repair jumped cancellations, missing symbol definitions, or unbridged matrix expressions via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
