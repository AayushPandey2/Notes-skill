# Subtemplate: Mathematical

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for theorems, derivations, quantitative relations, and proofs—building every prerequisite mathematical tool from scratch with small value tables, concrete failure checks, scalar-to-vector progression, and explicit step-by-step cancellations.
- **Activation conditions:** Displayed equations, formal derivations, proofs, quantitative modeling, or worked numerical problems (`mathematical`).
- **Expected inputs:** Governing equations, upfront **Symbol Dictionary** (with notation-clash warnings), local foundation tools (constants, logs, summations, derivatives), assumptions, full derivation transitions, and one canonical numerical fixture.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs at each conceptual inflection point. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Open with Goal/Prerequisites/TOC → concrete toy numbers + real-world uses (Layer 1) → complete **Symbol Dictionary** + notation-clash caveat (Layer 2) → plug numbers into a simpler rule to prove where it fails (Layer 3) → dedicated **Local Mathematical Foundations** section teaching every needed constant, log rule, sum, or derivative with tiny value tables and boundary plots (Layer 4) → progress **1-variable scalar case → multi-variable sum + contribution table → compact vector/matrix form with shape verification** (Layer 5) → dissect governing equations (`Part | Purpose` table), evaluate **negative / zero / positive / limiting cases**, and decode curve+derivative or geometric boundary visuals (Layer 6) → prove derivations step by step without hiding cancellations, noting **approximation vs. identity vs. theorem** (Layers 7–8) → carry one canonical fixture across a multi-step calculation/iteration (Layer 9) → test boundaries, counterexamples, and **what assumptions are NOT required** (Layers 10–11) → close with grouped Formula Cheat Sheet, Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced Q&A, step-by-step worked problems with sanity checks, and N-step Mental Model (Layer 12).
- **Domain expectations (Mathematics):** Prioritize definitions, assumptions, derivations or proofs, counterexamples, and generalization; explicitly distinguish **numerical evidence** from **proof**.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster plots at inflection points (foundation curves alongside tables, function + derivative pairs, before/after geometric transforms, and constraint regions).
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md); distinguish source derivations from added bridges; repair jumped cancellations, missing symbol clashes, or matrix expressions without scalar predecessors via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
