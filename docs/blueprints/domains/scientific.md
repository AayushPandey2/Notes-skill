# Subtemplate: Scientific

- **Purpose:** Ground natural-science laws, chemistry, biology, and engineering mechanisms in observable phenomena, physical/particle intuition, units, dimensional balance, and inspected figures.
- **Activation conditions:** Physical phenomena, chemical systems, biological mechanisms, experimental measurements, or equations carrying units (`scientific`).
- **Expected inputs:** Observable phenomenon, governing laws, physical constants/variables with SI or domain units, approximation regimes, experimental uncertainty bounds, and visual queries.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs of each substantive concept. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Teaching progression:** Start from the observable phenomenon and question → define coordinates, sign conventions, species/phases, and units → decode apparatus/structure/mechanism visuals via the six-phase visual narrative → derive or present governing laws with dimensional checks → work a unit-carrying example → separate idealized predictions from actual measurements and state validity limits.
- **Necessary prerequisite bridges:** Define every physical constant, coordinate axis, and unit immediately before first use; explain where idealized analogies (e.g., frictionless, ideal gas) break down.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using the six-phase visual narrative. Image opportunities: authentic apparatus photos, experimental setups, biological/physical structures, measured relationship plots, and verified chemical structures or particle-level representations.
- **Formalism appropriate to the knowledge type:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) with explicit SI/domain unit tracking, dimensional balance checks, and significant figures.
- **Worked-example requirements:** Solve one concrete numerical example carrying explicit units at every intermediate step, interpret the physical meaning, and test one regime limit where the model breaks down.
- **Evidence requirements:** Keep actual, supplied, or published measurements (with uncertainties) strictly separate from toy hypotheticals or predictions; never invent readings.
- **Practice and mastery checks:** Include prediction, unit/dimensional check, apparatus/figure-reading, and approximation-limit questions with collapsed answers.
- **Failure handling:** Locally repair unit mismatches, fabricated experimental curves, or mismatched figure notation via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
