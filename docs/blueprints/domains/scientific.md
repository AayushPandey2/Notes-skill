# Subtemplate: Scientific

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering theory by connecting observable phenomena, physical/particle models, units, governing laws, and approximation limits.
- **Activation conditions:** Physical phenomena, chemical systems, biological mechanisms, or laws carrying physical units (`scientific`).
- **Expected inputs:** Observable behavior, physical/chemical system boundaries, constants/variables with SI or domain units, 3-tier prerequisites, approximation regimes, and visual queries.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs of each substantive concept. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Start from an observable phenomenon and question (Layer 1) → intuitive physical/particle foothold with explicit idealization limits (Layers 2–3) → define coordinates, species, phases, and units (Layers 4–5) → decode apparatus, structural, or measured-relationship visuals (Layer 6) → trace causal mechanism and governing laws with dimensional checks (Layers 7–8) → work one unit-carrying example and interpret physical scale (Layer 9) → contrast competing regimes, failure of idealizations, and transfer to a second system (Layers 10–11) → check reasoning and unit consistency independently (Layer 12).
- **Domain expectations (Physics & Chemistry):**
  - *Physics:* Connect observable behavior, physical models, governing laws, units, predictions, measurement, and approximation limits.
  - *Chemistry:* Connect macroscopic observations, particle-level explanations, symbolic representations, reaction/equilibrium conditions, and quantitative reasoning.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster images for apparatus, physical/biological structures, measured curves, verified chemical structures, and particle-level diagrams.
- **Formalism, evidence & failure handling:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) with explicit unit tracking; keep actual/published measurements (with uncertainty) separate from idealized predictions; repair unit mismatches or unlabeled approximations via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
