# Subtemplate: Scientific

- **Purpose:** Realize the [12 teaching layers](../../../core/teaching_core.md) for physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering theory—using short 1–3 sentence paragraphs, concrete physical/particle tables, upfront symbol+unit dictionaries, zero-prerequisite math foundations, and inspected figures at inflection points.
- **Activation conditions:** Physical phenomena, chemical systems, biological mechanisms, or laws carrying physical units (`scientific`).
- **Expected inputs:** Observable phenomenon, concrete observation table, upfront **Symbol & Unit Dictionary**, local mathematical/dimensional foundations, approximation regimes, experimental uncertainty bounds, and visual queries.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs at each conceptual inflection point. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

- **Realizing the 12 Teaching Layers:** Open with Goal/Prerequisites/TOC → concrete observation table + real-world phenomena table (Layer 1) → upfront **Symbol, Coordinate & SI-Unit Dictionary** with convention-clash notes (Layer 2) → show numerically where a naive or overly idealized rule fails + contrast visual (Layer 3) → local mathematical/unit foundations needed for the law (Layer 4) → build from simple 1-body/1-species case → multi-component system → compact governing law (Layer 5) → break equation into parts (`Part | Physical Meaning`), check extreme limits (zero, small, large), and decode apparatus/particle/measured visuals (Layer 6) → derive mechanism and dimensional balance (`Starting state → interaction → intermediate change → equilibrium/outcome`) (Layers 7–8) → carry one unit-checked numerical fixture across multiple steps/conditions (Layer 9) → contrast validity regimes, state **"What idealizations this model drops vs. requires"**, and give `Common Error | Fix` table (Layers 10–11) → close with Governing Law Cheat Sheet, Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced Q&A, worked unit-checked problems, and N-step Physical Mental Model (Layer 12).
- **Domain expectations (Physics & Chemistry):** Connect observable behavior, physical/particle models, symbolic laws, units, predictions, measurements, and approximation limits cleanly.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster images for apparatus, setups, structures, measured curves, verified molecules, and particle-level diagrams.
- **Evidence & failure handling:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) with explicit unit tracking; keep actual/published measurements (with uncertainty) separate from idealized predictions; repair unit mismatches or unlabeled approximations via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
