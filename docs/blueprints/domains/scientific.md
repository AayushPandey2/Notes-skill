# Subtemplate: Scientific

- **Purpose:** Realize [teaching_core.md](../../../core/teaching_core.md) and [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) for physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering theory—using short 1–3 sentence paragraphs, concrete physical/particle tables, progressive symbol+unit introductions, zero-prerequisite foundations, and inspected figures at inflection points.
- **Activation conditions:** Physical phenomena, chemical systems, biological mechanisms, or laws carrying physical units (`scientific`).
- **Expected inputs:** Observable phenomenon, concrete observation table, progressive symbol & SI-unit introductions, local foundations, approximation regimes, uncertainty bounds, and visual queries.

- **Realizing the Conceptual Flow:** Open with separated `Difficulty / Prerequisites / Learning objectives` + clickable TOC → introduce one conceptual burden at a time with concrete observations → local foundations → early unit-checked example introducing symbols progressively → limitations of naive/overly idealized rules + contrast visual → concept-appropriate math depth per [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) (physical/chemical/biological quantities, conditions, causal reasoning, and limiting cases where they clarify the process, without forcing calculations into purely descriptive topics) → validity regimes, ideal-to-real progressions, `Common Error | Fix` table, visible Concept Checkpoints, concise synthesis, optional Notation Reference, and links to any generated companions.
- **Domain expectations (Physics & Chemistry):** Connect observable behavior, physical/particle models, symbolic laws, units, predictions, measurements, and approximation limits cleanly.
- **Visual opportunities:** Follow [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) using inspected local raster images for apparatus, setups, structures, measured curves, verified molecules, and particle-level diagrams.
- **Evidence & failure handling:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md) with explicit unit tracking; keep actual/published measurements (with uncertainty) separate from idealized predictions; repair unit mismatches or unlabeled approximations via [verification-checklist.md](../../procedures/review/verification-checklist.md).
