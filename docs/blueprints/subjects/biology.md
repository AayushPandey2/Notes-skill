# Subject Theory Blueprint: Biology

## Purpose

Teach Biology through **explicit scale selection, structure–function relationships, mechanistic vs. evolutionary explanations, dynamic regulation, and biological variability** aligned with [physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md).

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **Scale Selection & Nested Mechanisms:** Select the biological scale matching the topic (molecular, organelle, cellular, tissue/organ, organismal, population, ecosystem). Establish parent physiological/ecological purpose before detailing internal pathways, and return to system-level function.
2. **Mechanistic vs. Evolutionary Explanation:** Explicitly distinguish *proximate* mechanistic explanations (how enzymes, receptors, membranes, or gene circuits physically work) from *ultimate* evolutionary explanations (how selection, drift, mutation, and gene flow shape traits across generations—never treating individual physiological adaptation as evolution).
3. **Structure–Function & Energetics:** Explain *how* 3D molecular conformation, compartmentalization, and energy coupling (ATP hydrolysis, electrochemical gradients) drive function.
4. **Information Flow, Regulation & Variability:** Distinguish DNA/gene vs. allele, feedback regulation, cellular respiration vs. mechanical breathing, and **biological variation** (individual noise, environmental plasticity, penetrance) alongside quantitative model assumptions (e.g., Michaelis–Menten steady state, Hardy–Weinberg). Match formalism to the actual learning objective rather than defaulting to population-genetics algebra: for conceptual evolutionary, ecological, or physiological requests, prioritize biological structures, mechanisms, causal explanations, evidence, and misconception correction, adding mathematical models only when requested, essential to the central concept, or source-required—while preserving complete quantitative rigor whenever quantitative topics (Hardy–Weinberg, population dynamics, enzyme kinetics, quantitative genetics, biostatistics, biophysics) are taught.
5. **Evidence, Bio-Safety & Visual Mechanistic Teaching:** Separate experimental mechanism from correlation, never invent microscopy or assay data, and never supply unsafe biological agent protocols. Open with a concrete observable biological phenomenon before zooming into molecular pathways (closing needed chemistry or diffusion prerequisites inline without reducing biological theory to rote bulleted memorization), distribute verified web-sourced anatomical, cellular, molecular, or process illustrations per [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) (never generated/Mermaid/ASCII), and walk through one causal-perturbation example before an independent prediction task.
