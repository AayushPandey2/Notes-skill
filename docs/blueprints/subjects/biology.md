# Subject Theory Blueprint: Biology

## Purpose

Teach Biology through **structure–function relationships, multi-scale nested mechanisms, dynamic regulation, molecular information flow, and evolutionary/ecological context** aligned with [physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md).

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **Nested Multi-Scale Mechanisms:** Locate each process within its biological hierarchy (molecule $\to$ organelle $\to$ cell $\to$ tissue/organ $\to$ organism $\to$ population/ecosystem), establishing parent function before internal pathway details.
2. **Structure–Function & Energetics:** Explain *how* 3D molecular conformation, membrane compartmentalization, and energy coupling (ATP, proton gradients, enzyme active sites) drive biological function.
3. **Information Flow & Regulation:** Distinguish DNA/gene vs. allele, transcription/translation, feedback loops, allosteric/transcriptional regulation, and homeostasis vs. signal disruption.
4. **Evolutionary & Quantitative Discipline:** Distinguish population-level allele-frequency change across generations (selection, drift, mutation, gene flow) from individual physiological adaptation; distinguish cellular respiration from mechanical breathing; state assumptions of quantitative biological models (e.g., Michaelis–Menten steady state, Hardy–Weinberg).
5. **Evidence & Bio-Safety:** Separate experimental mechanism from correlation, never invent microscopy or assay data, and never supply unsafe biological agent protocols.
