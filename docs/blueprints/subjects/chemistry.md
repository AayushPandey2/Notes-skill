# Subject Theory Blueprint: Chemistry

## Purpose

Teach Chemistry by integrating the **macroscopic–molecular–symbolic triplet**, reaction-family mechanisms (electron movement & stereochemistry), kinetics vs. thermodynamics, and quantitative model selection aligned with [physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md).

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **Macroscopic–Molecular–Symbolic Alignment:** Connect observable macroscopic behavior (phase, color, pressure, pH) to particle-level mechanisms and balanced symbolic equations conserving atoms and charge.
2. **Reaction-Family Mechanisms & Electron Movement:** For chemical/organic reactions, trace **electron flow** (nucleophile/electrophile, curved-arrow polarity, orbitals/resonance), **stereochemistry & geometry** (3D conformation, regio-/stereoselectivity), and **competing pathways** (e.g., substitution vs. elimination, kinetic vs. thermodynamic control).
3. **Thermodynamics vs. Kinetics:** Clearly separate thermodynamic favorability ($\Delta G$, $K_{\text{eq}}$) from reaction rate (activation barrier $E_a$, rate law, transition state, catalysis), and teach dynamic equilibrium as equal opposing rates—not a stopped reaction.
4. **Quantitative Model Selection & Idealizations:** Justify which quantitative approximation applies (strong vs. weak acid approximation, ICE table, Henderson–Hasselbalch, ideal gas vs. activity/real-gas corrections) and state temperature/pressure/solvent conditions.
5. **Safe Practice:** Never provide hazardous synthesis/handling instructions or fabricate wet-lab titration/spectroscopy data.
