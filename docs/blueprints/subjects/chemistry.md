# Subject Theory Blueprint: Chemistry

## Purpose

Teach Chemistry by integrating the **macroscopic–molecular–symbolic triplet**, conservation laws, kinetics vs. thermodynamics, and safe experimental limits ([physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md)).

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **Macroscopic–Molecular–Symbolic Alignment:** Connect observable macroscopic behavior (color, phase, pressure, pH) to particle-level mechanisms (electron distribution, collisions, bond breaking/forming) and balanced symbolic equations.
2. **Conservation & Stoichiometry:** Enforce atom and charge conservation across every equation, tracking limiting reagents, states of matter, units, and significant figures.
3. **Thermodynamics vs. Kinetics:** Clearly separate thermodynamic favorability ($\Delta G$, equilibrium constant $K$, stability) from reaction rate (activation barrier $E_a$, rate law, catalysis), and teach dynamic equilibrium as equal opposing rates—not a stopped reaction.
4. **Idealizations & Conditions:** State temperature, pressure, solvent, and concentration/activity assumptions, showing where ideal-solution or ideal-gas models break down.
5. **Safe Practice:** Never provide hazardous synthesis/handling instructions or fabricate wet-lab titration/spectroscopy data.
