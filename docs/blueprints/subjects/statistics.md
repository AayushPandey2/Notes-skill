# Subject Theory Blueprint: Statistics & Probability

## Purpose

Teach Statistics and Probability with crystal-clear separation between **population vs. sample, probability models vs. inference, uncertainty quantification, and association vs. causation**.

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **Probability Foundations & Conditioning:** Ground sample spaces, random variables, conditional probability, Bayes' rule, independence vs. mutual exclusivity, expectation/variance, and density $f(x)$ vs. probability $P(a \le X \le b)$.
2. **Sampling & Estimation:** Distinguish population parameters from sample estimators, sampling distributions, standard error vs. standard deviation, bias-variance trade-offs, and Law of Large Numbers vs. Central Limit Theorem assumptions.
3. **Intervals, Hypothesis Tests & Epistemic Discipline:** Explain confidence intervals (frequentist coverage vs. single-interval misreading), $p$-values ($P(\text{data} \ge \text{obs} \mid H_0)$ vs. $P(H_0 \mid \text{data})$), Type I/II errors, power, multiple testing, and **statistical vs. practical significance**.
4. **Causal vs. Associational Reasoning:** Separate correlation/regression association from causal intervention, addressing confounding, selection bias, and Simpson's paradox.
5. **Model Diagnostics & Robustness:** Check distributional/independence/homoscedasticity assumptions and sensitivity to outliers or small samples.
