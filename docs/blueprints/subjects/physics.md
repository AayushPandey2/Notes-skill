# Subject Theory Blueprint: Physics

## Purpose

Teach Physics through **physical systems, reference frames, conservation laws, ideal-to-real model progression, limiting-case checks, and measurement uncertainty** aligned with [physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md).

## Core Theory Obligations

1. **System Boundary & Conventions:** Define the system of interest, surroundings, coordinate axes, reference frame, and sign conventions before writing equations.
2. **Governing Laws & Physical Mechanism:** Distinguish fundamental laws (conservation of energy/momentum/charge, Newton's laws, Maxwell's equations, thermodynamic laws) from empirical constitutive relations (Hooke's law, Ohm's law, ideal gas law).
3. **Ideal-to-Real Progression:** Explicitly state simplifying idealizations (point mass, frictionless surface, small angle, ideal gas), then show how relaxing them (drag, non-inertial terms, van der Waals interactions) changes the model and introduces new regimes.
4. **Dimensional, Limiting & Qualitative Checks:** Check SI units at every stage, distinguish scalars vs. vectors and state functions vs. path quantities (heat vs. temperature, work, mass vs. weight, velocity vs. acceleration, energy vs. power), and test extreme limits ($m \to 0$, $v \ll c$, $\theta \to 0$).
5. **Experimental Honesty:** Separate theoretical predictions from measured data and uncertainty propagation; never invent physical apparatus observations.
