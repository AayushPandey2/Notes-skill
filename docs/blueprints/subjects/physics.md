# Subject Theory Blueprint: Physics

## Purpose

Teach Physics through **physical systems, reference frames, conservation laws, ideal-to-real model progression, limiting-case checks, and measurement uncertainty** aligned with [physical-lab-safety.md](../../procedures/safety/physical-lab-safety.md).

## 1. Core Theory Obligations

1. **System Boundary & Conventions:** Define the system of interest, surroundings, coordinate axes, reference frame (inertial vs. non-inertial), and sign conventions before writing equations.
2. **Governing Laws vs. Equations of State & Constitutive Relations:** Distinguish fundamental laws (conservation of energy/momentum/charge, Newton's laws, Maxwell's equations, thermodynamic laws) from empirical constitutive relations (Hooke's law, Ohm's law) and **idealized equations of state** (such as the ideal gas law $PV=nRT$, distinguishing ideal point-particle/no-interaction assumptions from real-gas behavior).
3. **Ideal-to-Real Progression:** Explicitly state simplifying idealizations (point mass, frictionless surface, small angle, ideal gas), then show how relaxing them (drag, non-inertial terms, van der Waals interactions) changes the model and introduces new regimes.
4. **Dimensional, Limiting & Qualitative Checks:** Check SI units at every stage, distinguish scalars vs. vectors and state functions vs. path quantities (heat vs. temperature, work, mass vs. weight, velocity vs. acceleration, energy vs. power), and test extreme limits ($m \to 0$, $v \ll c$, $\theta \to 0$).
5. **Experimental Honesty:** Separate theoretical predictions from measured data and uncertainty propagation; never invent physical apparatus observations.

## 2. Phenomenon-Specific Branches (Activate Matching Branch)

- *Mechanics:* Free-body diagrams, reference frames, work-energy vs. impulse-momentum, rotational dynamics.
- *Thermodynamics:* System/control-volume boundary, state vs. process variables, First/Second Laws, entropy & irreversibility.
- *Electromagnetism:* Charge/current sources, fields vs. potentials, Gauss/Faraday/Ampère-Maxwell laws, boundary conditions.
- *Waves & Optics:* Wave equation, superposition/interference, boundary reflection/refraction, diffraction limits.
- *Quantum Mechanics:* State vectors/wavefunctions, operators/observables, measurement probabilities, commutation & uncertainty.

## 3. Physical Intuition, Web-Sourced Visuals & Practice
Develop physical intuition from an observable everyday phenomenon and close prerequisite vector/calculus tools inline before writing formal governing equations. Distribute verified web-sourced diagrams of physical systems, forces, fields, apparatus, or experimental spectra per [representations.md](../../procedures/authoring/representations.md) (with before/image/caption/after teaching; never generated/Mermaid/ASCII), and progress from one unit-checked symbolic-and-limiting-case worked example to an independent prediction/problem check.
