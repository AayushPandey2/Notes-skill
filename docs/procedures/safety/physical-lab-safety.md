# Physical & Wet-Lab Safety Policy

## Purpose

Ensure Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Electrical Engineering notes and companion labs stay safe, honest, and grounded when Scriptorium cannot perform physical experiments or when apparatus/materials involve hazards.

## Safety & Scope Boundaries

1. **Low-voltage and non-hazardous physical realizations only:** Physical educational realizations in Electrical Engineering and Physics must remain strictly within safe, low-voltage, low-energy educational conditions (e.g., benchtop 5 V DC resistor/RC networks, small-angle pendulum timing). Mains electricity, high-voltage/high-current power systems, ionizing radiation, lasers, and high-energy mechanics require professional supervision and must be modeled analytically, simulated, or studied from published data instead.
2. **No unsafe wet-lab or biological procedures:** In Chemistry and Biology, never provide step-by-step synthesis or handling instructions for hazardous, toxic, explosive, controlled, or biohazardous materials, and never encourage unsupervised manipulation of pathogens or biological agents. Use conceptual molecular mechanisms, stoichiometry/equilibrium calculations, safe educational simulations, or established published datasets.
3. **Never fabricate apparatus observations:** Distinguish **Analytical**, **Hand-calculated**, **Illustrative**, **Expected**, and **Published** results from **Observed** apparatus readings ([experimental-lab.md](../authoring/experimental-lab.md)). Never invent lab measurements, oscilloscope traces, titration readings, or microscopy results.
