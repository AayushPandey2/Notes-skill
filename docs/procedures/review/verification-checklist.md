# Semantic Verification Checklist

## Purpose & Activation Trigger

Perform an explicit manual review of reader-facing teaching quality, source fidelity, representation alignment, lab completeness, and Markdown/Obsidian structure before concluding any note-creation or update task. Review the actual generated content, not merely headings. **Do not treat heading count, word count, or image count as proof of teaching quality.** This is a manual review checklist, not an automated software test or validator script.

## Reader-Facing Quality Questions

Before finishing any substantive theory note or lab, verify every applicable prose question honestly (**never use word count, heading count, or image count as a measure of teaching depth**):
1. Does the opening introduce **one conceptual burden at a time** (never stuffing advanced claims into an opening callout), separate `Difficulty`, `Prerequisites`, and `Learning objectives` with blank lines before a **clickable Obsidian TOC** (`[[#...|...]]`), use fewer **question-driven** `H1–H4` headings (no 1-sentence micro-sections or bold pseudo-headings), keep definitions concise beside concrete examples, place comparison tables *after* explaining compared ideas, and use **zero `==highlight==`, semantic spans, or `\color{...}`**?
2. Is every essential prerequisite taught locally before dependent concepts, are transformations (e.g., probability → odds → log-odds) and multi-step objectives (e.g., MLE) motivated step by step with concrete numbers before general notation, and is the complete Notation Reference placed at the **end** (with essential notation defined locally first)?
3. Is one consistent **running example / system / dataset** carried through an early intuitive walkthrough, formalism, and **mechanism-interpreted** traces (explaining *why* each transition happens and interpreting the outcome—for example, in ML: residual signs, feature multiplication, averaging, sign-flipping updates, and why total loss can drop while individual points worsen; in CS/sciences: state transitions, physical/biological mechanisms, and failure diagnostics), while consolidating core mechanics before extensions?
4. Does every concept use the minimum mathematical profile (`Conceptual` through `Formal reasoning`) justified by its objective, build the explanatory bridge before each equation, never skip central transitions (`"rearranging gives"`), pair every **top-level** displayed `$$...$$` block with `> [!example]- Understand this formula step by step` (without recursive nested folds for equations already inside a formula/lab/answer fold), format derivations in `aligned` and piecewise rules in `cases`, and distinguish reasoning types?
5. Are **relevant, inspected local web images** (typically 3–5 for visual/geometric/empirical/video topics, 0–2 for purely symbolic or compact notes, and **disabled under `strict` source-only mode**) embedded with controlled width (`|650`), separated by blank lines from a concise 1-sentence caption and **ordinary Markdown** clickable source + verified attribution/license line (zero raw HTML `<span>` tags), checked against the actual saved asset before describing colors/labels/values, never falsely claiming an external figure uses the running dataset or proves generalization from visual smoothness alone, and **zero Mermaid blocks or ASCII graphs**?
6. Are tables focused on **one question** (no 7-column overloaded tables or paragraph-length cells), with numerical columns right-aligned (`|---:|`), semantic outcome labels (`0 — Fail`, `1 — Pass`), selected/winning rows marked clearly (without `==highlight==`), inputs separated from outputs, one hand-worked candidate shown *before* comparison tables, and exact loss/probability terminology?
7. Are training and prediction phases separated explicitly, toy simplifications declared honestly, theoretical results separated from library defaults (`scikit-learn` probability averaging vs. hard voting, `max_features`, finite-sample vs. asymptotic OOB) and empirical observations, strong claims flagged and qualified (no `"independent trees"` for correlated ensembles, no `"always"` / `"safer"` blanket advice), hyperparameters framed around decisions, and visible `> [!question] Concept Checkpoint` blocks aligned with the stated learning objectives?
8. Do comprehensive theory notes end with a final **Mental Model**, trailing **Notation Reference**, and reciprocal links to the sibling lab and sibling **Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A** file?
9. Are sources, citations, and execution status (`Source and static review only; not compiled or executed here.`) honest, with zero filler or ornamental images?
10. **Adversarial Theory Review & Concept Mastery:** For substantive theory notes, apply the six adversarial lenses and 0–7 concept-level benchmark in [adversarial-theory-review.md](adversarial-theory-review.md) plus [theory_quality_contract.md](../../../core/theory_quality_contract.md) (progressive abstraction, explanatory/analytical completeness, forward & reverse reasoning, ideal-to-real transitions, epistemic claim status, and concept-to-application mapping), repairing missing high-priority mechanisms locally rather than padding prose.

## Structural, Visual & Lab Gates

- **Direct-Authoring & Safety:** Confirm no scripts, downloaders, or builder files were created or executed, and no existing unrelated Obsidian notes or assets were overwritten.
- **Metadata & YAML:** Line 1 starts with valid `--- ... ---` YAML matching either the 5-key comprehensive `M1` profile (`id`, `aliases`, `topic`, `tags`, `nexttopics`) or Obsidian Properties (`topic`, `tags`, `master_topic`, `domain`, `note_type`), with zero `type/<note-type>` tags, zero leaked scaffold comments, and zero internal planning logs.
- **Mathematical Formatting, Delimiters & Operator Parity:** Follow [math_gates.md](../../../core/math_gates.md): use native `$...$` (short symbols/relations only) and separate-line `$$...$$` with blank lines before/after, `aligned` derivations, `cases` piecewise definitions, and `> [!example]- Understand this formula step by step` beneath every **top-level** displayed `$$...$$` block (non-recursive inside existing folds; every interior line prefixed with `>`). Reject `\(...\)` / `\[...\]`, code fences for equations, raw LaTeX in prose, excessive boxing, `==highlight==` / `\color{...}`, unbridged opening equations, repeated symbol dictionaries, wrong equation-to-code operators (`*` vs `@`, `**` vs `^`), or unedited self-correction transcripts.
- **Visual Coverage & Local Acquisition:** Verify relevant downloaded & inspected local web raster images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`) per [representations.md](../authoring/representations.md), each placed at a conceptual inflection point and taught with the full six-phase narrative. Respect `strict` source-only precedence (no external web images required or permitted). Reject planned/remote image URLs without local raster assets, images inserted without six-phase prose, or **any Mermaid (`mermaid` blocks) or ASCII graphs/diagrams**.
- **Substantive Explanations, Companion Lab & Cheatsheet/Q&A Pack (3-File Bundle):** Reject patronizing shortcuts (`"obviously"`, `"trivially"`, `"as you know"`), visible opening inventory dumps, obsolete standalone lab chapters, forced optimizers, mismatched objective scaling or operators, missing **A–G** collapsed walkthroughs directly beneath lab implementations, or unverified runtime claims. Confirm each new substantive non-video learning note (`M1`, `M2`, `M5`; exempting compact `M3`/`atomic-card`, standalone `M4`/`M6`/`M7`, and `/scribe-ytnotes`) has both sibling companions—`<note stem> - Experimental Lab.md` (with `Source and static review only; not compiled or executed here.`) and `<note stem> - Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A.md` (with every interview answer in `> [!tip]- Answer` and every practice solution in `> [!tip]- Solution`, properly quoted and separated)—all linked reciprocally and aligned to the active YAML profile.
- **Links, Source Coverage & Safe Saving:** Internal `[[wikilinks]]` resolve to existing or planned notes, all fenced code blocks are closed, citations have actual locators, and supplied source coverage gaps or unverified steps (execution, hashing, Obsidian UI rendering) are disclosed honestly. Inspect written files after saving and confirm unrelated files remain intact.
- **Video Notes (`/scribe-ytnotes`):**
  - *Source fidelity:* Sections reflect actual video narration/transcript, timestamps match URL `t=` seconds (supporting hours), substantial added context is labeled (**Additional explanation**), and missing access is never fabricated.
  - *Visual teaching:* Multiple complementary downloaded & inspected web raster images (`assets/scriptorium/<video-id>/`) illustrate the video's concepts, every embed resolves locally with source attribution, and visuals are taught with the six-phase narrative (no video-frame capture required).
  - *Organization:* Exactly one self-contained note per processed video (single videos never split by topic), playlist order/links are preserved when requested, and **zero** experimental lab is generated.
  - *Learning quality & efficiency:* Essential prerequisites are explained locally, labels/symbols are decoded, mechanisms are traced, formalism connects to the visuals, video playback/frame capture was avoided, the transcript was acquired once into one compact evidence map, and repairs are targeted. Mark the output **partial** only if transcript/source access itself was incomplete.
