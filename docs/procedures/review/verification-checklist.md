# Semantic Verification Checklist

## Purpose & Activation Trigger

Perform an explicit manual review of reader-facing teaching quality, source fidelity, representation alignment, lab completeness, and Markdown/Obsidian structure before concluding any note-creation or update task. Review the actual generated content, not merely headings. **Do not treat heading count, word count, or image count as proof of teaching quality.** This is a manual review checklist, not an automated software test or validator script.

## Reader-Facing Quality Questions

Before finishing any substantive theory note or lab, verify every applicable prose question honestly (**never use word count, heading count, or image count as a measure of teaching depth**):
1. Does the opening separate `Difficulty`, `Prerequisites`, and `Learning objectives` with blank lines before a plain-text TOC, use short navigable `H1–H4` headings (no bold pseudo-headings), and keep paragraphs to 1–3 sentences with controlled bolding?
2. Is every essential prerequisite (base models, math tools) taught locally *before* dependent formulas, with symbols introduced **progressively** and the complete Symbol Dictionary placed at the **end**?
3. Are early intuitive worked examples shown before full derivations, and is the **same running dataset** carried consistently through derivations, candidate comparisons (e.g., candidate splits), classification mechanics (if claimed), and evaluation?
4. Are composite equations split into components, derivations formatted as multiline `aligned` blocks (not single-line `\implies` chains), long expressions placed in `$$...$$` rather than dense inline math, core formulas highlighted, and mathematical **meaning** explained visibly without `> [!note]- 📐 Understand this formula` folds or `"Read aloud"` filler?
5. Are **at least 4–5 relevant, inspected local web images** placed at conceptual inflection points, explained in calm takeaway-driven prose (no commanding `"Inspect Figure..."` phrasing), with concise 1-line captions separated from muted attribution, and **zero Mermaid blocks or ASCII graphs**?
6. Are tables compact (no 5–6 column wide tables or paragraph-length cells), with numerical columns right-aligned (`|---:|`), inputs visually separated from calculated outputs, and exact loss terminology (e.g., SSE vs half-SSE)?
7. Are assumptions paired with *"What this method does NOT require"*, `Mistake | Correct approach` tables, inline **Concept Checkpoints** after major sections, and qualified statements (zero unsupported absolutes and **zero unedited self-correction transcripts** like `"Wait—check the sign"` or retained wrong tables)?
8. Do comprehensive theory notes end with a final **Mental Model**, trailing **Notation Reference**, and reciprocal links to the sibling lab and sibling **Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A** file?
9. Are sources, citations, and execution status (`Source and static review only; not compiled or executed here.`) honest, with zero filler or ornamental images?

## Structural, Visual & Lab Gates

- **Direct-Authoring & Safety:** Confirm no scripts, downloaders, or builder files were created or executed, and no existing unrelated Obsidian notes or assets were overwritten.
- **Metadata & YAML:** Line 1 starts with valid `--- ... ---` YAML matching either the 5-key comprehensive `M1` profile (`id`, `aliases`, `topic`, `tags`, `nexttopics`) or Obsidian Properties (`topic`, `tags`, `master_topic`, `domain`, `note_type`), with zero `type/<note-type>` tags, zero leaked scaffold comments, and zero internal planning logs.
- **Mathematical Formatting & Visible Core Derivations:** Use native `$...$` (short symbols only) and separate-line `$$...$$` / multiline `aligned` blocks without nested delimiters. Reject `\(...\)` / `\[...\]`, single-line `\implies` derivation chains, hidden core derivations inside collapsed theory callouts, mechanical `"Read aloud"` / `> [!note]- 📐 Understand this formula` boilerplate, unedited self-correction transcripts, or retained wrong calculation tables.
- **Visual Coverage & Local Acquisition:** Verify **at least 4–5 downloaded & inspected local web raster images** (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`) per generated content/deep/study/video note, each placed at a conceptual inflection point and taught with the full six-phase narrative. Reject notes with fewer than 4 downloaded web images (unless web/download access was genuinely blocked and disclosed), planned/remote image URLs without local raster assets, images inserted without six-phase prose, or **any Mermaid (`mermaid` blocks) or ASCII graphs/diagrams**.
- **Substantive Explanations, Companion Lab & Cheatsheet/Q&A Pack (3-File Bundle):** Reject patronizing shortcuts (`"obviously"`, `"trivially"`, `"as you know"`), visible opening inventory dumps (`Level`, `Estimated prerequisites`, `Implementation languages`, `Libraries and tools`, `Practical objective`, `Experiment objective`), obsolete standalone lab chapters (repeated theory, historical background, general investigations, 12-step workflows, quizzes/challenges, roadmaps, or repetitive summaries), forced optimizers, mismatched objective scaling or operators (`*` vs `@`, `**` vs `^`, int vs float division), missing **A–G** collapsed walkthroughs (`> [!example]- Understand this implementation: mathematics, code, and numerical dry run`) directly beneath lab implementations, four ML implementations solving incomparable tasks, or omitting Rust-library code merely because local Cargo/compiler access is absent. Confirm each new non-video content note has both sibling companions—`<note stem> - Experimental Lab.md` (with `Source and static review only; not compiled or executed here.`) and `<note stem> - Cheatsheet & Interview Q&A.md` (with every interview answer in `> [!tip]- Answer` and every practice solution in `> [!tip]- Solution`, properly quoted and separated)—all linked reciprocally.
- **Links, Source Coverage & Safe Saving:** Internal `[[wikilinks]]` resolve to existing or planned notes, all fenced code blocks are closed, citations have actual locators, and supplied source coverage gaps or unverified steps (execution, hashing, Obsidian UI rendering) are disclosed honestly. Inspect written files after saving and confirm unrelated files remain intact.
- **Video Notes (`/scribe-ytnotes`):**
  - *Source fidelity:* Sections reflect actual video narration/frames, timestamps match URL `t=` seconds (supporting hours), substantial added context is labeled (**Additional explanation**), and missing access is never fabricated.
  - *Visual teaching:* Multiple complementary downloaded & inspected web raster images (`assets/scriptorium/<video-id>/`) illustrate the video's concepts, every embed resolves locally with source attribution, and visuals are taught with the six-phase narrative (no video-frame capture required).
  - *Organization:* Exactly one self-contained note per processed video (single videos never split by topic), playlist order/links are preserved when requested, and **zero** experimental lab is generated.
  - *Learning quality & efficiency:* Essential prerequisites are explained locally, labels/symbols are decoded, mechanisms are traced, formalism connects to the visuals, video playback/frame capture was avoided, the transcript was acquired once into one compact evidence map, and repairs are targeted. Mark the output **partial** only if transcript/source access itself was incomplete.
