## Mathematical, scientific and source audit

- **Heat capacity:** The G r2 rule is consequentially false without the capacity-constancy qualification. The general integral and fixed-amount/molar distinction above independently establish the repair target. H instructs preservation of such assumptions but has no generated artifact demonstrating success.
- **Michaelis–Menten:** For $K_M=200\,\mu\mathrm{M}$, $K_d=150\,\mu\mathrm{M}$, $[S]_0=200\,\mu\mathrm{M}$ and $V_{\max}=10\,\mu\mathrm{M/s}$, the $K_M$ denominator gives $5.000\,\mu\mathrm{M/s}$; substitution of $K_d$ gives $2000/350=5.7142857\,\mu\mathrm{M/s}$, 14.29% higher relative to the first prediction. This compares predictions, not QSSA validity or an ODE error bound. The historical G note's “better than 0.01%” phrase is not proved by its small-parameter estimate alone. Molarity-based biochemical units are conventional, whereas strict SI concentration is $\mathrm{mol\,m^{-3}}$; “exact” for the tight-binding quadratic means exact for the algebraic quasi-steady balance, not the full time-dependent solution. These are artifact-level qualification findings, not H production edits.
- **Numerical tables:** F's Catalase quotient $10^7/(2.5\times10^{-2})=4\times10^8$, ten times its reported $4\times10^7$. Existing `core/math_gates.md` already explicitly covers derived table columns/ratios and an independent exponent/order-of-magnitude check. G's omission of the row is no evidence of mechanism repair. Behavioral evaluation remains required.
- **Oscillator:** The historical G transfer note calls $O(\Delta t)$ a local Euler error. For ordinary smooth solutions, the unnormalized one-step local state error is $O(\Delta t^2)$ and accumulated global error is $O(\Delta t)$. A normalized local residual convention would need to be named. This is an artifact-level correction if published; no unrelated runtime edit was made.
- **Strict source:** `source-fidelity.md` requires retaining supplied examples and prohibits invented examples or external lookup under R-S0. The pre-existing G Ohm fixture had no worked example. Its observed G output did not invent one, but output text alone cannot establish zero external tool calls. H's new behavior on a strict source *with* a worked example is not yet observed.
