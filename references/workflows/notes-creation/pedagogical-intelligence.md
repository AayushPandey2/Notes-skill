# Pedagogical Intelligence & Learning Experience Layer

Load this only when a deep-study note needs additional explanation guidance. [Adaptive planning](adaptive-engine.md) chooses concept depth, the [subtemplate registry](instructional-registry.json) maps knowledge types, the [module guide](../../templates/modules/module-catalog.md) lists conditional teaching moves, and [research guidance](../research/research-engine.md) handles source checks. This page adds focused explanation principles (`P01`–`P11`).

## 1. Selective Activation & Ownership

Activate only the capabilities justified by a concept's learning objectives, audience, and depth profile. Standalone definitions, historical narratives, and nontechnical essays omit irrelevant coding, tensor-shape, or diagram machinery cleanly.

1. **P01 — Cognitive Dependency Mapping:** Separate the prerequisite graph $G=(V,E)$ from chapter hierarchy. Label edges `essential`, `supporting`, `alternative_route`, `contextual`, or `future_application`, and assign readiness-sensitive treatments (`assume`, `remind`, `bridge`, `teach`, `defer`). Order independent curricula topologically on `essential` edges; resolve cycles deliberately (split overbroad concepts or downgrade mutual links to `contextual`). Under `strict`/`expanded` source-order constraints, preserve source sequence and insert concise local prerequisite bridges before use.
2. **P02 — Progressive Explanation Layers (`L1–L8`):** Conditionally sequence `motivation` (L1), `intuition` (L2), `mechanism` (L3), `formal_definition` (L4), `formalization` (L5), `worked_application` (L6), `limitations` (L7), and `integration` (L8) at `omitted`/`minimal`/`standard`/`detailed`/`deep` levels. Introduce unfamiliar ideas progressively without forcing every layer onto every concept.
3. **P03 — Multiple Representation Alignment:** Keep prose, equations, algorithms, numerical examples, diagrams, tensor shapes, units, and code semantically and mathematically consistent via a canonical concept specification (`symbols`, `tensor_shapes`, `units`, `equation`, `expected_values`). Disclose where any analogy breaks down. If representations conflict, repair the defective representation against the authoritative definition (never silently altering a supplied source under `strict` mode).
4. **P04 — Worked-Example Intelligence:** Choose examples by pedagogical role (`minimal`, `worked`, `representative`, `boundary`, `counterexample`, `failure`, `debugging`, `transfer`, `comparative`) rather than arbitrary quotas. Consolidate redundant examples teaching the exact same mechanism without adding a new boundary, failure mode, or complexity level. Distinguish `source_provided`, `source_adapted`, `illustrative`, and `researched` provenance.
5. **P05 — Visual Reasoning & Diagram Selection:** Use a figure, table, or native Mermaid diagram only when it clarifies a relationship costly to reconstruct in prose. Follow [representations.md](representations.md), inspect labels, axes, units, and arrows, and omit decorative or misleading visuals. Do not generate HTML/JavaScript viewers.
6. **P06 — Misconception Anticipation:** Place targeted corrections (`terminological`, `mathematical`, `conceptual`, `implementation`, `statistical`, `scope`) at the **point of need** where confusion arises (optionally summarized briefly later). Never assert that a specific reader holds a misconception without evidence, and never invent misconception frequency statistics.
7. **P07 — Educational Quality Review:** Identify defects (`critical`, `major`, `moderate`, `minor`) with concept ID, location, defect type, and minimal local repair rather than full-note regeneration ([verification-checklist.md](../validation/verification-checklist.md)).
8. **P08 — Cross-Topic Knowledge Integration:** Connect concepts across existing vault notes using typed semantic relationships (`prerequisite_of`, `builds_upon`, `applies`, `generalizes`, `special_case_of`, `alternative_to`, `contrasts_with`, `shares_foundation`, `shares_implementation`, `historically_from`, `used_together_with`) resolved against existing vault notes. Distinguish `existing_note`, `planned_note`, `external_resource`, and `unresolved_concept`; write concise conceptual bridges instead of duplicating prior chapters or turning every technical term into a link.
9. **P09 — Adaptive Detail & Cognitive Load Control:** Allocate concept-specific `detail_profile` facets (`minimal`, `standard`, `detailed`, `deep`, `exhaustive`) without arbitrary word quotas. Define symbols near first use, chunk multi-step derivations, introduce scalar examples before vectorized notation for beginners, and separate optional advanced derivations while preserving full required coverage and source fidelity.
10. **P10 — Executable Learning & Verification:** Follow the planned coding depth (`C0–C5`) strictly—never force code into non-computational subjects. Map equations explicitly to code variables/lines, never execute untrusted retrieved source code, and label unexecuted code `Status: static review (not executed)` unless a native tool actually ran it.
11. **P11 — Multi-Layer Revision & Navigation:** Keep **Layer A** (full learning note) authoritative; layer **Layer B** (structured concept review: core definitions, key formulas with applicability conditions, critical distinctions) and **Layer C** (rapid revision or linked `cheat-sheet`) without introducing unsupported new claims, stripping mathematical assumptions, or duplicating full paragraphs verbatim.

## 3. Explanation Intelligence System (`EIS-01`–`EIS-07`)

Every concept explanation uses one shared `ExplanationBlueprint` structure:

1. **`EIS-01` — Explanatory Spine Construction:** Build a domain-adapted reasoning thread (`Problem → Necessary Insight → Mechanism → Result → Implication`) without inventing false causality; preserve major source sequence with concise local transitions in source-order mode.
2. **`EIS-02` — Central Question Reconstruction & Anticipation:** Identify the central intellectual question each concept answers and selectively anticipate follow-up questions to sequence prose naturally (avoiding FAQ heading spam and keeping questions minimal/implicit in `M3` cheat sheets).
3. **`EIS-03` — Causal Mechanism & Derivation Motivation:** Explain *why* significant mathematical operations are performed and what information each step produces; distinguish local first-order arguments from global convergence guarantees and avoid empty algebraic narration.
4. **`EIS-04` — Applicability Boundaries & Conceptual Contrasts:** Separate necessary vs. sufficient conditions, state failure boundaries, prevent heuristic-as-guarantee overclaims, and provide discriminating contrasts (e.g., parameter vs. hyperparameter, correlation vs. causation) without caveat overload on simple definitions.
5. **`EIS-05` — Variable Roles, Invariants & State Narration:** Explain operational variable roles, verified numerical/interval state traces, and valid invariants only where applicable—never forcing execution traces or invented invariants onto nonprocedural concepts.
6. **`EIS-06` — Retrieval Cues & Reconstruction Summaries:** Provide concise reasoning-preserving cues and reconstruction summaries that retain critical assumptions (kept internal-only in question-only `M6` assessments; avoiding multi-summary inflation).
7. **`EIS-07` — Reader Clarity Review (`CF-01`–`CF-12`):** Read the draft as the intended learner, check where prior knowledge or a step is missing, and repair within the source policy. This is an author's judgment, not measured mastery.

Never expose internal blueprint labels (`Explanatory Spine:`, `Starting Problem:`, `Anticipated Questions:`, `Reader Simulation`) as visible note headings.
