### Authoring contract

- **Paper / Context:** Record title, authors, publication/preprint date, venue if verified, canonical URL/identifier, version studied, and accessible supplementary material/code. Distinguish preprint and published versions when differences matter.
- **What problem was it trying to solve?** Explain the pre-paper bottleneck, strongest relevant baseline, existing workaround, and why the problem mattered. Use the paper and verified historical sources rather than hindsight alone.
- **Main idea:** State the central contribution in plain language, what was actually new, required assumptions, and what was inherited. Avoid repeating promotional claims as established novelty.
- **How it works:** Explain the mechanism or argument step by step with a named example, architecture/data-flow diagram when useful, and relationships to prior approaches. Do not assume the reader already understands unexplained notation from a figure.
- **Math / Technical Details:** Apply the correct math level. At level 2, derive an essential result with assumptions and full symbol anatomy, clearly labeling an explanatory reconstruction. At level 1, explain the needed equations and worked use. At level 0, rename this heading to **Technical Details** and explain substantive non-math details; do not include an empty math module. State which claims are proved, empirical, or heuristic.
- **Experiments / Results:** Record evaluation question, dataset/task, baselines, metrics and direction, setup, compute where available, and the key results with exact table/figure/page references. Preserve units and whether results are means, single runs, or reported with uncertainty.
- For ablations, record **Component changed | What stayed fixed | Metric/result | What this supports | What it does not establish**. State when no ablation or sufficient setup is provided. Do not manufacture experiments for a theoretical paper; explain its theorem, proof evidence, or validation method instead.
- Separate reported numbers from independently reproduced observations. An optional minimal reproduction must follow the shared verification rules, including environment and honest execution status.
- **Limitations / Open Questions:** Separate the authors' stated limits from your analysis. Include assumptions, external-validity concerns, failure cases, missing evidence, and specific unresolved questions. Avoid asserting causal conclusions that the study design does not establish.
- **What changed after this?** Explain later influence or successors only when verified. If later work was not researched, state that scope limit instead of inventing a lineage. Bridge to the next concept worth studying.
- **Related notes / Sources:** Link the original paper at exact sections/equations/figures, supplements, official code, and verified related work. Suggest bounded Atomic/DeepStudy follow-ups without automatic recursive creation.
