### Authoring contract

- **Idea:** State exactly one idea in clear language. Include its essential formula or relationship only when applicable. Define its terms and validity condition within the note; do not rely on an unexplained symbol from a parent note.
- **Example:** Give one tiny numerical or concrete real-world anchor that demonstrates the idea. Keep it small enough to inspect mentally.
- Include a compact direct source citation where the claim needs attribution. Do not bury a research-sized source list inside an atomic note.
- **Connections:** End with these three explicit relationship slots: **Parent / Broader**, **Contrasts With**, and **Used In**. Choose semantically honest targets. Resolve existing notes; use plain-text `Suggested: <concept> — not created` where no valid note exists. Never fabricate a contrast merely to fill a slot.
- Keep the entire rendered body **under 150 words**, including headings, example, citation labels, and connection block; exclude YAML and non-rendered Markdown syntax. Use the same documented word-count convention as the Cheat-Sheet.
- A proof requiring significant space belongs in a linked deep note. The atomic note retains the result and its necessary conditions.
- Use canonical titles/paths to prevent duplicate atoms. Do not recursively generate a graph of new notes from its connection slots.
