# Variant: Formula Sheet

- **Parent Master:** [M3 — Quick Reference & Cheat Sheet](../masters/quick-reference.md)
- **Specialization:** High-density mathematical and physical equation reference.
- **Key Overrides:**
  1. **Required per formula:** Canonical equation (`$$...$$`), inline symbol definitions (with units/dimensions/shapes), explicit **applicability conditions & assumptions**, and one-line interpretation.
  2. **Safety constraint:** Never list naked formulas stripped of domain restrictions, smoothness/convexity hypotheses, or special cases (`validate_revision_integrity`).
  3. **Source-only compatibility:** When extracted from a supplied PDF under `R-S0`, include only equations present in the source with exact equation numbers/locators.
