# Subtemplate: Scientific

- **Purpose:** Ground natural-science laws, chemistry, and engineering mechanisms in physical intuition, dimensional balance, approximation regimes, and inspected figures.
- **Activation conditions:** Physical phenomena, chemical systems, biological mechanisms, experimental measurements, or equations with units (`scientific`).
- **Required inputs:** Observable phenomenon, governing laws, physical constants/variables with units, approximation conditions, experimental bounds, and visual queries.

> **Authoring-agent image instruction:** Before drafting, identify the visual learning needs of each substantive concept. Acquire suitable images where they materially improve understanding. Inspect the saved images, then compose the explanation around their actual visible content. Do not treat image placement as an optional finishing step.

## Teaching Sequence

1. **Observable phenomenon:** Motivate the physical or chemical behavior in everyday terms before introducing laws or equations.
2. **Apparatus, structure & units:** Define coordinates, sign conventions, species/phases, and SI or domain units before first use.
3. **Visual-led mechanism:** Acquire and inspect needed figures, then walk through labels, apparatus, or molecular structure using **Orient → Show → Explain → Work through → Connect**.
4. **Governing laws + fold:** Present equations with dimensional balance checks and `> [!note]- 📐 Understand this formula` callouts per [math_gates.md](../../../shared/math_gates.md).
5. **Worked calculation & validity limits:** Compute a concrete unit-checked example and separate observed measurements from idealized assumptions.

## Obligations

- **Evidence expectations:** Separate actual, supplied, or published measurements (with uncertainties) from idealized predictions; never invent readings.
- **Visual requirements:** Follow [representations.md](../../workflows/notes-creation/representations.md) using **Orient → Show → Explain → Work through → Connect**. Image opportunities: physical apparatus, experimental setups, biological/physical structures, mechanisms, measured relationship plots, and verified chemical structures or particle-level representations.
- **Mathematical explanation requirements:** Enforce [math_gates.md](../../../shared/math_gates.md) with explicit SI/domain unit tracking and dimensional consistency checks in every worked calculation.
- **Practical-example requirements:** Include one complete unit-carrying numerical example and one boundary check where the idealization breaks down.
- **Failure handling:** Reject inconsistent units, fabricated experimental curves, or mismatched figure notation; acquire an alternative source or disclose unmet visual needs.
- **Completion checks:** Verify against [verification-checklist.md](../../workflows/validation/verification-checklist.md) — unit balance, figure attribution/decoding, and valid regime boundaries.
