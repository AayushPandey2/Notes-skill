# Why circle area changes with radius

## What it is

The two-dimensional radial measure is governed by the quintessential circumference identity. For a circle of radius 1, area is 3.14.

## Why it matters

$$
\frac{dA}{dr}=2\pi r.
$$

> [!example]- Understand this formula step by step — Derivative
>
> Differentiate the area formula.
>
> The radius is the distance from the center to the boundary. The derivative measures area gained per unit increase in radius. When the radius increases by a small amount, the new area lies in a thin ring. The original circumference provides the leading contribution.
