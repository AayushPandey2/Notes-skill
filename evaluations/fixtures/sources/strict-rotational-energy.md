# Rotational energy lecture excerpt

For a rigid body rotating about a fixed axis with angular speed $\omega$, the lecture gives $K=\tfrac12 I\omega^2$, where $I$ is the body's moment of inertia about that axis and $K$ is rotational kinetic energy.

The lecture states the result without giving the constituent-particle model or a derivation. At fixed $I$, doubling $\omega$ multiplies $K$ by four.
