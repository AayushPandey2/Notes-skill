# Evaluation Rubric: Theory Quality (Evaluation-Only)

*Load only during evaluation or when explicitly requested as an evaluation rubric—do not load during normal note authoring. Apply only criteria relevant to the intended artifact (`M1`/`M2` theory vs. compact `M3` vs. strict-source note) rather than penalizing intentionally omitted material.*

Every criterion requires **citing exact passages** from the generated note. Author self-attestation (e.g., *"I verified this checklist"*) never counts as passing evidence.

| Dimension | Pass Standard (Cite Supporting Passage) | Automatic Fail Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **Concept Depth (Unified 0–5)** | Core concepts achieve target depth (`Level 3–5`: mechanism, assumptions/limits, and transfer/reverse reasoning) | Core mechanism reduced to bare `Level 1–2` mention/definition |
| **Visible Mathematical Flow** | With folds closed, the reader can trace the objective, assumptions, visible transformations and result through connected prose and equations | Essential milestone or assumption available only in a fold, or displays form a formula catalogue |
| **Derivation Completeness & Mathematical Validity** | Consequential relationships have justified derivations in descriptive folds, worked solutions or canonical references, with identities established before use and applicable checks | Missing or invalid derivation, dimension or normalization error, or a stipulated definition presented as a theorem |
| **Progressive Abstraction** | Concrete example/case precedes formal notation where helpful, or definition-first / theorem-first order is justified cleanly with immediate concrete grounding | Opens unfamiliar concept with unexplained abstract equation and zero grounding |
| **Factual & Disciplinary Accuracy** | Definitions, theorems, equations, and misconceptions match authoritative subject truth (e.g., TLB miss vs. page/permission fault, test-statistic $p$-value, qualified $R+W>N$) | Any factual error, wrong sign/operator, or unqualified overclaim |
| **Domain Neutrality & Scope** | Uses primary subject's natural structure without forcing ML datasets/losses onto unrelated subjects | ML terminology, classification metrics, or irrelevant boilerplate leaked into non-ML note |
| **Source & Epistemic Integrity** | Honors active `source_policy` permissions; distinguishes definitions, models, approximations, hypotheses, and gaps | Fabricated citations, unpermitted external browsing under `strict`, or invented visual claims |
| **Conceptual explanation** | Purpose, local prerequisites, and a consequence are taught at the requested depth independently of formal derivations | A technically correct definition or topic mention substitutes for an explanation |
| **Mechanism and transfer** | Core objectives have a reconstructible trace or causal argument, a useful worked example, and an application check when required | A topic mention or a recall question replaces the required mechanism, diagnosis, or transfer |
| **Beginner Accessibility & Opening Progression** | Opens with plain-language intuition, concrete scenario, and simpler baseline before dense formalism; teaches required mathematical tools before applying them | Opens with a jargon-heavy reference summary assuming unintroduced prerequisites or invokes untaught tools/methods |
| **Curriculum Completeness (`M1`)** | Comprehensive `M1` note covers foundations/baselines, core mechanism, key task types/variants, practical configuration, limitations, comparisons, and primary references proportionally | `M1` note degenerates into a narrow formula sheet or condensed reference omitting major central pillars |
| **Visual Necessity & Acquisition** | Includes visually inspected diagrams/plots for structural, geometric, sequential, or functional concepts when permitted, or documents attempted acquisition and honest fallback | Zero visuals on a visual-heavy topic with no disclosed capability or source constraint |
